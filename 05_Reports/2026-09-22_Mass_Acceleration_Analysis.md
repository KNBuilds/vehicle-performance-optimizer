# Mass vs Acceleration Investigation

Date: September 22, 2026

## Research Question

How does vehicle mass affect acceleration when driving force is kept constant?

## Model

F = ma

Therefore:

a = F/m

## Constant

Driving force = 3000 N

## Variables

Independent variable:
Vehicle mass

Dependent variable:
Acceleration

## Results

![Alt text describing the graph]("E:\GAP YEAR 2026-2027\01_VECHICLE _PERFORMANCE_OPTIMIZER\02_Code\plot_mass_acceleration.py")


## Observation

As vehicle mass increased, acceleration decreased.

## Explanation

For a constant driving force, increasing mass reduces acceleration because acceleration is equal to force divided by mass.

## Assumptions

- Driving force remains constant.
- Vehicle mass remains constant during each test.
- Road gradient is ignored.
- Aerodynamic drag is ignored.
- Rolling resistance is ignored.
- Tyre slip is ignored.
- Drivetrain losses are ignored.

## Limitations

This is a simplified model and does not represent the complete acceleration behaviour of a real vehicle.
